# Dark, driving acoustic indie — short-form Sonora

Original dark Spanish-language acoustic indie with a raw, emotionally direct performance. Dry rhythmic nylon/steel-string guitar, tense low bass, organic percussive accents and a subtle forward pulse. Moderate energy and momentum, not a slow lullaby. Intimate vocal with natural human grain and expressive phrasing; clear, native-sounding Spanish and French.

Aim for a **45–60 second finished song**. Start the sung verse almost immediately (0–3 seconds), no long instrumental intro, no spoken introduction, no teaching instructions, no "listen to the French words". Keep the whole song coherent, with a small memorable hook. Two short French lyrical interventions continue the same story and illuminate target meanings, not a glossary or literal line-by-line translation. Brief breathing room around language changes, but maintain rhythmic flow.

Avoid cute/twee, cozy, bubbly, whimsical or sugary production. No "boom-boom" kick-snare loop, no toy-like plucks, no big theatrical build, no motivational-pop chorus. Do not make vocals artificially slow. Keep the arrangement lean enough that each target word remains intelligible.

This is an original composition; do not imitate any particular performer, recording or melody.
