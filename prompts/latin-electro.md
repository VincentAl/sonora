# Minimal Latin / Electronic — Suno Prompt

Minimal Spanish-language Latin electronic pop. Deep soft bass, airy synths, restrained percussion with subtle Latin rhythmic influence, hypnotic groove, and an intimate close vocal.

Keep the arrangement spacious and background-work friendly. Slow, clear vocal delivery despite the groove. Sparse lyrics and deliberate gaps between phrases.

Spanish is dominant. French appears only in one or two short standalone lines, separated clearly from Spanish by a small musical pause. French lines should be natural lyrical thoughts containing the exact target meaning, never dictionary-style translations.

French pronunciation should be as native and clear as possible, with minimal Spanish accent. Avoid rushed code-switching.

Subtle, sensual, restrained, not club-heavy, around 1 minute.
