# Soft Indie Pop — Suno Prompt

Soft Spanish indie pop with dreamy synths, clean electric guitar, warm bass, restrained electronic drums, and an intimate close vocal. Bittersweet, elegant, understated, and calm enough for background listening while working.

Very slow vocal phrasing, sparse lyrics, and plenty of breathing room. Avoid dense verses and rushed syllables.

Spanish is the dominant sung language. French appears only in one or two short standalone lyrical breaks. Each French line should feel like its own image or thought, not a translation or vocabulary definition. Leave a noticeable pause before and after French passages.

French pronunciation should sound natural and native-like, with minimal Spanish accent. Do not rush bilingual transitions.

Catchy enough to remember, but never theatrical or overly busy. Around 1 minute.
