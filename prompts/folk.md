# Intimate Folk — Suno Prompt

Intimate Spanish-language Latin indie folk with subtle French passages. Warm nylon guitar, soft piano, light acoustic textures, delicate organic percussion, and very restrained bass.

Close-mic female vocal, tender, natural, slightly melancholic, with a lot of breathing room. Slow to mid tempo. Very slow vocal phrasing, sparse lyrics, long pauses between lines. No dramatic belting, no dense production, no overly catchy pop chorus.

Spanish is the main sung language. French appears only in very short standalone lines, clearly separated from the Spanish, like reflective interludes rather than translations. Leave a noticeable pause before and after each French line.

French pronunciation should sound natural and as native as possible, with minimal Spanish accent. Do not rush the bilingual transitions.

Poetic, intimate, and calm enough to play while working. Around 1 minute.
