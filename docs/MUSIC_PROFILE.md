## Strongest inferred preferences

### 1. Intimacy over spectacle

Prefer close, human, emotionally direct performances over large cinematic production.

Useful traits:
- close-mic vocal;
- restrained dynamics;
- audible breathing room;
- organic imperfections;
- sparse arrangements;
- emotional tension without belting.

### 2. Acoustic / organic core

A recurring axis is voice + guitar / piano / organic accompaniment rather than dense electronic production.

Useful palette:
- nylon or acoustic guitar;
- dry piano;
- restrained bass;
- room ambience rather than huge reverbs;
- percussion only when it adds character rather than a cute pop pulse.

### 3. Melancholic, vulnerable writing — but never "cute"

The target mood can be intimate, bittersweet, fragile, wounded, sensual, raw, or quietly intense.

Avoid:
- cheerful educational-song energy;
- cartoonish positivity;
- motivational-pop uplift;
- oversized anthemic choruses;
- twee / "mimi" indie-pop;
- bouncy kick-snare "boom boom" accompaniment;
- plucky ukulele-like or toy-pop textures;
- overly sweet, cozy, whimsical arrangements.

### 4. Spanish and Iberian / Latin texture

The references tolerate a wide range inside Spanish-language music:
- Latin indie folk;
- singer-songwriter;
- acoustic pop;
- contemporary Spanish indie;
- restrained flamenco / folk influence;
- raw regional or traditional texture.

This is broader than generic "Latin pop".

### 5. Modern production, but not polished to death

Electronic elements are allowed only if they support the vocal and preserve tension.

Useful electronic traits:
- dry or muted beat;
- dark low-end;
- sparse synth texture;
- asymmetric / textural percussion;
- low-density arrangement.

Avoid making "soft", "minimal" or "background-friendly" synonymous with cute, cozy, rounded pop.

## Revised generation families

### A. Bare intimate acoustic

Voice-forward, nylon/acoustic guitar, almost no percussion, close and emotionally exposed.

### B. Dark indie acoustic-pop

Acoustic foundation plus restrained modern bass/drums, melancholic hook, dry production, no bouncy or sugary pulse.

### C. Raw Iberian / flamenco-adjacent

Dry guitar, sparse hand percussion or palmas-like texture, raw vocal phrasing, earthy timbre.

Do not imitate a named flamenco performer or existing song.

### D. Minimal nocturnal electronic

Sparse, dark beat, warm low-end, tense/airy texture, intimate vocal. Avoid cute syncopation or soft-pop bounce.

### E. Fragile piano / chamber folk

Piano-led, very light acoustic texture, slow phrasing, emotionally exposed. Avoid sentimental "pretty" scoring.
